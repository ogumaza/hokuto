Hokuto 2.1.0 adds an audio mixdown, three effects of the Switch, bank changes on DSE and support for Linux through Wine. It also makes the songs of Quintet's GBA driver play their sampled channels, which 2.0.0 left silent.

The Windows build requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. Its C++ runtime is linked in. The N and KN editions of Windows require Microsoft's Media Feature Pack too. The Mac build requires macOS 11 or later on Apple Silicon. Neither build is signed with a certificate. The README's "Download" section says how to open them the first time. On Linux, run the Windows build under Wine 10 or later. Linux users can also build a native program from source, as a preview.

## What's new

- **Audio mixdown.** File > Export > Audio Mixdown… (Ctrl+B) renders songs to WAV files offline, through the engine that plays them and much faster than real time. It exports the whole mix, a stem for each track, or both. A song that loops plays its loop twice and fades over 10 s by default, or renders a seamless loop that the WAV file marks for samplers. The same window exports songs as MIDI files with SoundFont 2 or SFZ instruments, and several exports can wait in a queue. On the command line, `hok mixdown` renders WAV files and `hok midi` exports MIDI files.
- **The Switch's effects.** The mixer's FX channels offer three more effects on the Switch: the delay, the light limiter and the reverb of `nn::atk`, the Switch's sound library. They play as the console plays them, in strict and in enhanced mixing. Ctrl-click resets one of their parameters to the library's default.
- **Banks on DSE.** The Bank menu lists the banks that a DSE sound loads, and a track can play another of them. Hokuto adds bank selects around the track's notes. It refuses a change where a new bank select would reset the track's bend range, LFOs or envelope, and says which commands set them.
- **DS sequence archives.** The sound effects in a DS archive's sequence archives (SSAR) now take edits that change their size. Each record of the archive moves with its bytes.
- **Linux.** Hokuto's Windows build runs under Wine 10 or later. Under Wine, it draws its text with the system's fonts and plays the AAC streams of 3DS games through FFmpeg. Ubuntu 26.04 is the only distribution tested. A native Linux program builds from source too. It's a preview: `docs/development.md` gives the steps and what has been tried.
- **Drivers under their consoles.** The mixer's engine switch shows each driver under its console. For a DSE file, the DS opens a menu of DSE and NITRO-Composer. Project Setup gives the console and its sound library or driver.
- **Saves.** On macOS and Linux, a save through a symbolic link keeps the link and writes the file it points to. A save keeps the file's permissions. A read-only or locked file is no longer overwritten: Hokuto says so and points to File > Save As….
- **Bank edits in the background.** A bank edit runs while the window keeps drawing. The editors show grey until it settles. A click on Add, Duplicate or Delete in the meantime waits, and then makes its edit.
- **The format.** Hokuto 2.1.0 writes version 1.2 of the `.hok` format. Version 1.2 adds two settings of the Switch's archive player, the sequences of a DS archive's sequence archives and the Switch's three new effects. `docs/format.md` specifies it. Hokuto 2.0.0 opens a file of version 1.2 without the two settings, refuses a file with one of the new effects, and can't play or export a DS file that holds a sequence archive.
- **The interface.** The windows that show while a file opens or an import is prepared, and other windows that fit their height to their text, open in the middle of the main window. Names that don't fit end with an ellipsis, in the browser and in menus such as the Player menu, and the row's tooltip gives the whole name. A slider's marker no longer covers its value. The browser's Sound section stays open or closed after a restart. When Hokuto starts at another display scale than the one its layout was saved at, it lays out the zones again for that scale.

## Fixes since 2.0.0

- On Quintet's driver, both sampled channels were silent in every song. They now play.
- Some sound effects of The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Anniversary Edition and Spirit Tracks closed Hokuto. They now play.
- In Four Swords Anniversary Edition, BGM_SND426 stopped after about half a second. It now plays to its loop.
- In Spirit Tracks, the pieces that the game plays in a fixed mix of its tracks, such as SEQ_34, SEQ_38 and SEQ_45, play in that mix.
- On Nintendo R&D2's driver, a note that the driver cuts off no longer plays its release on. Super Mario Advance 4 had it.
- DSE: Luminous Arc 2 and Professor Layton and the Diabolical Box play on the builds of the driver that the games hold. Two songs of Luminous Arc 2 that failed to play now play. The DSE songs of Fushigi no Dungeon: Fuurai no Shiren DS 2 open.
- A MIDI export of a DS sequence that the sound archive gives a volume of 0 says that the game plays it silent.
- On Quintet's driver, a tempo edit could move the loop of a song's marker row. It no longer does.
- On some builds of Konami's driver, the inspector refused to give a track a wave instrument. The edit now goes in.
- The engine switch, Another Console and File > Import left out the notes of some tracks of Rare's driver. The tracks that play one MIDI channel now share its settings, through loops and drum pairings.
- A bank sent to the DS could take more memory than its source. Hokuto now lowers the sample rates of the largest waves first, one at a time, and only as far as the bank requires. Smaller waves keep their rates.
- The model form refused a Switch archive whose player uses advanced wave sounds or a sequence sound countdown. It now keeps both settings.
- A GBA note that you audition, in the Key Editor or the browser, plays with the reverb, volume and channels that the game's code gives its song. Before, it took the first song's.
- A typed `.aiff` or `.midi` stays in an export's name. Before, `song.aiff` became `song.aiff.aif`.
- The WAV export of a wave archive makes its folder when the folder doesn't exist yet. Before, it failed at the first file.
- The refusal to replace a file that shares its place in the archive with other data is shorter, and no longer suggests importing the archive again.
- The count of a DSi image's stale hashes groups its digits.
- The sources build with newer C++ libraries, such as Visual Studio's STL 14.52 and libstdc++ 15.

## Known limitations

- The GameCube isn't supported yet.
- An imported archive plays without the game's effects. The game's code sets them up. Add them in the mixer.
- A game's code can change what a sequence plays, through its variables, track mutes and volumes. Hokuto plays the values that the Variables zone sets.
- The Wii plays in strict mixing only.
- MusicPlayer2000 has no sends, and a tied note takes only a new velocity. You can't add songs to the song table.
- Konami's driver has no sends or comparisons, and its songs have no tempo. On revisions without a track pan, the pan knob refuses.
- On Konami's driver, a volume command can start a PSG note again at the key of the note command before it. After a silent note command, each volume command shows as a note, and where several take their key from one note command, the Key Editor can't move one or give it a new key: drag its point in the volume lane, or edit the commands in the List Editor. After an audible note command, the notes that volume commands start again show as one note with it: a new key changes them all, and a new velocity changes only the first. A new or moved note can't go between a note command and a volume command that starts it again, and a volume point can't move past a note command.
- On Nintendo R&D2's driver, a sound effect takes no edit of its tracks.
- On Brownie Brown's driver, the fader refuses a PSG track's volume and the pan knob refuses every pan. A new length has to be a sum of the driver's lengths.
- Ubisoft Milan's driver has no volume, pan or sends. Only the notes of a piece's first sequence can be edited.
- Rare's driver has no pan or sends, and a tune keeps its count of tracks.
- On Quintet's driver, a note can't move or change its length, and it has no velocity. A new note goes only on a rest, in the octave that the channel plays there, and only where it would sound as a separate note. A setting can't move off its tick, and a new one can't go inside a note. A song keeps its 6 tracks, and there's no pan or sends.
- On Krawall, the mixer and the inspector refuse their edits. A row's volume and instrument change in the List Editor. A note sits on its row and can't move. Modules that games load from packed music files play and export, but can't be edited.
- With the GBA's drivers other than MusicPlayer2000 and Konami's, the Bank Editor shows the samples without editing them, Replace… takes no songs or samples, and File > Import opens whole ROMs only. On Konami's driver, Replace… takes samples but no songs, and File > Import opens whole ROMs only. GBA songs export as MIDI with SoundFont 2, and only those of Konami's driver export as SFZ too.
- NITRO-Composer has no sends, and a track volume stops at 127. DS sequences open as programs, never as timelines.
- DSE has no sends. File > Import adds nothing to DSE files yet, and a DSE bank can't come from a SoundFont 2 or SFZ file.
- An export into a DSi-enhanced cart can't sign it again. A DSi in DSi mode may refuse the image.
- No game or emulator has played some kinds of edit yet. The user guide says which ones.
- The models of the consoles start a wave sound or a stream at once, without the game's loading.
- A DSE game whose build of the driver no model covers plays on the model of the later build, without a warning.
- `hok lengths` measures a Switch sequence that sends to an `nn::atk` delay to its limit of 10 minutes.
- On Windows, restart Hokuto after you change the display scale.
- Opening another file while an audio mixdown runs stops the export without a message.
- Under Wine, Hokuto opens Wine's file dialogs. They don't remember the last folder, and they add the filter's extension to a name that already has one. Once, after hours, a dialog never showed, and Hokuto took no clicks until Wine stopped. The README gives the details.
- The native Linux program is a preview. A message box there has no parent window: a click on the main window can raise it over the box, and Hokuto then ignores input until the box closes.
